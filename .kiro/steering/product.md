# Product Overview

posMe (v4posme_maui) is a mobile point-of-sale (POS) and customer management application targeting Android devices. It is used by field sales agents and retail operators to manage day-to-day commercial operations.

## Core Capabilities

- Invoicing and billing with multi-step workflow
- Payment collection (cash and Pagadito credit card gateway)
- Credit management and amortization schedules
- Customer management and visit tracking
- Inventory/item catalog browsing with barcode scanning
- Receipt printing via Bluetooth thermal printers
- Data synchronization with a remote REST API backend
- Closing reports and sales summaries
- GPS-based location tracking for field visits

## Business Context

- The app operates offline-first with a local SQLite database and syncs data with a centralized server
- Currency handling includes exchange rate support (Córdobas / USD)
- The product URL is https://posme.net
- App identifier: `com.posme.v4posme_maui`
- Language: the UI and domain terminology are primarily in Spanish
